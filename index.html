More than a game list

Everything you need to play with friends is built in.

Play online

14 games put you in a room with people on the website, the app and itch.io. When nobody else is there, bots fill in.

Locked friend chat

Chat with friends and parties of up to 12. Messages are end-to-end encrypted, and bad words are blurred.

Touch or controller

Every game works with a phone screen, keyboard and mouse, or a gamepad. Change the buttons, stick size and look speed.

Works offline

Install Blockyard as an app and it keeps a copy for when you have no internet. It updates by itself.

Build your own worlds

Studio lets you build 3D worlds out of blocks, add simple scripts, test them and publish them for others.

Tested every day

Test bots play every game on laptop and phone sizes each day and report anything broken. See today's report.

Get the app

Free on every device. The app opens full screen, works offline, and updates itself.

Android

⬇ Download for Android
  1. Tap the button and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Tap Install. Blockyard shows up with your other apps.

Or with no download: open this page in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Install app.

iPhone & iPad

Open Blockyard
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Windows

⬇ Download for Windows
  1. Click the button and open blockyard.exe when it finishes.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Blockyard opens in its own window. It plays offline too.

On a Mac or Chromebook: open Blockyard in Chrome or Edge and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

Prefer itch.io? Blockyard is on itch.io too.
