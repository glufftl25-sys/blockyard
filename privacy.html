Blockyard Arcade Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Blockyard Arcade is a free arcade of block games that runs in your browser, as an installed app, on itch.io and on Google Play. This page explains what the game saves about you, where it is kept, and how to delete it.

What we save

  • Your account. When you make an account, we save your player name and password with Google Firebase Authentication. Firebase stores the password securely; we never see it. If you choose to sign in with email instead, your email address is saved there too.
  • Your game progress. Your bricks, avatar, worlds, tasks and best scores are saved to your account in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), so you can sign in on another device and keep playing. Only your own account can read or change your save.
  • On your device. The game also keeps your progress and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password in your browser's storage, so it works offline.
  • Online games. When you play an online game, your player name, avatar colour and what you are doing in that game (like where your player is) are shared with the other players in the same game while you play. This is removed when you leave. Online play uses an anonymous Firebase sign-in that is not linked to your name or email.
  • Requests. If you use the Ask button to send a request to the game's maker, the request and your player name are sent, locked so that other players cannot read them.

What we do not do

  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking tools.
  • We never sell or share your information with anyone.
  • No chat with strangers on the website, app, itch.io or Google Play versions.

Other services

The game uses Google Firebase to keep accounts, saves and online games, and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may see your device's internet address when they are used, under Google's privacy policy. Tips on itch.io are optional and handled entirely by itch.io.

Children

Blockyard is made to be fun and safe for everyone. Players only need a made-up player name and a password; no real name, email or photo is needed. If you are a child, ask a grown-up before making an account, and before leaving a tip on itch.io.

Delete your account

In the game: open Blockyard, go to Settings, find your account, and tap Delete my account. Type your password to confirm. This deletes your online account and all its saved progress, on every device, straight away.

Can't get into the game? Open a request on GitHub with your player name, and the maker will delete your account and data.

You can also delete what is saved on one device by clearing the site's data in your browser or uninstalling the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date. Questions? Ask on GitHub.
